Who walks through the Life and Home doors

Audience insights for Lighthouse's two acquisition front doors: who they are, what they watch and read, what they believe about money, and how to buy them within the special ad category rules. Every number comes from the MRI-Simmons data haul and compares each door's audience with all U.S. adults.

How to read

Bars show the share of each door's audience. The grey tick is the share of all U.S. adults.
141Index = audience share ÷ U.S. adult share × 100. 100 is average; 120+ is a real skew.
⇅Switch the audience cut for either door in the bar at the top. Every chart, persona stat and targeting table updates.
*Fewer than 50 respondents. MRI flags these as unstable; read them as directional.

07 · Making it buyable

What we can actually set in each platform

This is the second pass on the audience work. We start from what each platform lets us target, then go back into MRI for traits that fit those settings. Meta and Google won't let us target age on either door, so we target the life they've built: their advisers, their giving, their home projects.

3

Behavioral layers for The Trade Desk

A signal counts when it indexes 125 or higher, reaches at least 7% of the cut and comes from 80 or more respondents. Signals from 60 to 79 respondents count as directional and carry a †. A layer is a Buy when two or more of its signals pass, a Test when one passes and a Skip when none do. Health and money-trouble signals are never used for targeting.
